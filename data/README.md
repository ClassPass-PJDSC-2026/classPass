# Data

- `raw/`: source files as obtained. The two TeacherPH files were copied by hand from a web table (see below).
- `processed/`: generated by `make data` (`presets.csv`). Safe to delete and regenerate.
- All data is public and aggregated (region and level). No student-level or school-level records.

School years differ between files (enrollment 2020-2021, schools 2020-2021, classrooms 2019-2020). `scripts/make_presets.py` (#8) records both years in its output.

## psa_enrollment.csv
- **Note:** the filename is historical. This is **DepEd** enrollment data republished by TeacherPH, not a PSA table.
- **Source:** TeacherPH, "DepEd Basic Statistics on Classroom-Learner Ratio", section "DepEd Enrollment Statistics for School Year 2020-2021 (Public)". Original data: DepEd Learner Information System as of 2021-06-28.
- **URL:** https://www.teacherph.com/deped-basic-statistics-classroom-learner-ratio/
- **Page dates:** published 2021-08-30, modified 2022-07-23.
- **Accessed:** 2026-10-23
- **School year:** 2020-2021
- **Filters:** public schools; Elementary, Junior High School, Senior High School; all 17 regions
- **License/terms:** not stated for the data. Secondary republication of DepEd statistics; site terms at https://www.teacherph.com/terms-conditions-of-use/. Used for non-commercial academic purposes.
- **Provenance:** transcribed by hand from the web table. Spot-checked by <name> on <date>.
- **Loading:** `pd.read_csv("data/raw/psa_enrollment.csv")` (wide format: one column per level).
- **Known issues:** secondary source, not the primary DepEd file; region labels use Roman numerals ("Region IV-A"); CARAGA is upper case.

## psa_schools.csv
- **Source:** Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), OpenSTAT, "TABLE 10.10 Number of Public and Private Schools by Region and Level of Education: SY 2019-2020 to SY 2023-2024" (source agency: DepEd)
- **URL:** https://openstat.psa.gov.ph/PXWeb/pxweb/en/DB/DB__3S__C10/0151C2BSCH1.px/?rxid=fc22bdb2-3b2f-439e-afe1-d39e1055d8da
- **Accessed:** 2026-10-23
- **School year:** 2020-2021
- **Filters:** Type of School = Public; Elementary, Junior High School, Senior High School; all 17 regions
- **License/terms:** CC BY 4.0; credit PSA or the source agency. Terms: https://openstat.psa.gov.ph/Terms
- **Loading:** `pd.read_csv("data/raw/psa_schools.csv", sep=";", skiprows=2, encoding="utf-8")`. The export has a title line and uses semicolons. Region names start with ".." (for example "..NCR") and need stripping.
- **Known issues:** public counts include State and Local Universities and Colleges; region names differ from the DepEd files ("Ilocos Region", "MIMAROPA Region", "Caraga"); school counts differ slightly from those on the TeacherPH page (for example NCR elementary 519 vs 516).

## deped_classrooms.csv
- **Source:** TeacherPH, "DepEd Basic Statistics on Classroom-Learner Ratio", section "DepEd Number of Classrooms (Public) for School Year 2019-2020". Original data: DepEd National School Building Inventory (NSBI), SY 2019-2020.
- **URL:** https://www.teacherph.com/deped-basic-statistics-classroom-learner-ratio/
- **Page dates:** published 2021-08-30, modified 2022-07-23.
- **Accessed:** 2026-10-23
- **School year:** 2019-2020 (not 2020-2021)
- **Filters:** public schools; Elementary, Junior High School, Senior High School; all 17 regions
- **License/terms:** not stated for the data. Same as above.
- **Provenance:** transcribed by hand from the web table. Spot-checked by <name> on <date>.
- **Loading:** `pd.read_csv("data/raw/deped_classrooms.csv")` (wide format).
- **Known issues:** secondary source; the page's elementary total (468,624) is one more than the sum of its regions (468,623); the page labels the classroom total column "Grand Total Enrollment", which is a typo on the page; classroom year (2019-2020) is one year earlier than enrollment.
